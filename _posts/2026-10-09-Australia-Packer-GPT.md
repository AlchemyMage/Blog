# 你要怎麼「遠端投 → 確定 → 飛」

你的 CV 最上方不要只寫 Melbourne，直接把 relocation 意願變成賣點，例如：

> **Based in Melbourne, VIC | Available to relocate anywhere in Australia**  
> **Available from: [日期] | Can relocate by air at short notice**  
> **Valid Australian work rights – [你的 visa subclass]**  
> **Australian mobile number | TFN obtained | Australian bank account ready**  
> **Seeking seasonal harvest / packing / packhouse / production work**

但是 **TFN number 本身絕對不要寫進 CV**，銀行帳號也不要。只需要說 *TFN obtained / Australian bank account ready*；真正 onboarding 被正式雇用後再提供。

你投 application 時，cover message 要非常短，重點不是寫人生故事，而是讓 recruiter 一眼看到：

Hi, I'm currently based in Melbourne and looking for seasonal packing, packhouse or harvest work anywhere in Australia.

I have valid Australian work rights ([visa type]), an Australian TFN and bank account, and I am available to relocate by flight at short notice. I am happy to work early starts, weekends, long shifts and in regional areas, and I can commit until [date].

I'm particularly interested in packing shed, sorting, grading, production or harvest roles. I am physically fit, reliable and available to start from [date].

My resume is attached. If you have a position available, I would be happy to complete a phone/video interview and relocate once the start date and location are confirmed.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Australian mobile number]

這個 message 可以直接丟 **email、SEEK application、Indeed、仲介 contact form、Facebook recruiter DM**。

## 一個很重要的策略：不要問「有沒有工作」，要問「哪裡現在缺人」

打仲介電話時直接說：

> “I'm in Melbourne but I'm willing to relocate anywhere in Australia. I'm looking for packing, packhouse, harvest or production work. I can fly interstate once a start date is confirmed. Do you have any immediate-start or upcoming positions?”

然後馬上補四個資訊：**visa + available date + 可以待多久 + 有沒有車/駕照**。

這比「Hi, I'm looking for farm job」有效很多。Recruiter 腦袋裡其實是在 match vacancy；你讓他知道 **QLD/NSW/NT/TAS/SA/WA 全接受**，他才有機會把你塞進別州缺人的 site。
